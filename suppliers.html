Suppliers

Suppliers

Thank you for your interest in working with GRB SUBSEA LTD.

Our supply partners are an important part of building energy’s future. That’s why we proudly partner with suppliers who share our purpose, values and commitments to advance our global sourcing strategies, together. 

 

The resources below provide insights into our requirements and guide you through the process to determine if your business is a good fit.

Partner With Us

We value our relationships with suppliers and recognize the role they play in the success of our projects. If your business meets the requirements of our Supplier Code of Conduct and documents listed below, please join our supplier network. 

Supplier Code of Business Conduct

Our suppliers share our purpose, values and commitments. And expectations are high to deliver results for our customers safely, efficiently and on-schedule. Think you fit the bill? First ensure you meet the expectations outlined in our Supplier Code of Conduct.
