Our Story

Forging Possibilities

More than a century ago, the combination of an emerging industry and the bond of family brought life to GRB SUBSEA LTD. Every day since, we’ve forged a path of endless possibilities with determination, perseverance and an unwavering commitment to solving the world’s energy challenges. As we look to the future, we rely on innovation paired with our proven expertise to empower a brighter, more sustainable future.

Developing Talent

Through a range of development opportunities, employees are empowered to build unique experiences at every career stage, while expanding technical skills and leadership capabilities. Explore growth and development programs at GRB SUBSEA LTD.

Our Culture

Life at GRB SUBSEA LTD

Here, what you do matters. Our employees think big and embrace the important role they play in building energy’s future. We proudly work together, across borders, toward one vision - to create and deliver complete, innovative solutions as the trusted global partner to our customers. The unique perspectives and voices across the company propel us forward, and we strive to cultivate an environment where every employee feels valued, respected, and recognized. 

A Global Resource, Local Force

We call many places home, and we work together across oceans, as one global team, to concept and construct the structures that transport and transform oil and gas into the products we need every day. Our global presence provides access to the resources necessary for success: our people, our facilities, and our ﬂeet of specialty marine construction vessels.

GRB SUBSEA LTD Awards

2025 Top Workplaces Award

Houston Chronicle

We are named in the Houston Chronicle Top Workplaces for companies with 500 or more employees in Houston, reflecting the teamwork and dedication of the people that make GRB SUBSEA LTD a place where employees thrive.

Excellence Award - Engineering

Malaysia International Business Awards 2025

We took home the Excellence Award in the Engineering category by transforming our KL office into a global engineering hub and growing from 30 to over 1,000 experts, proving our commitment to world-class EPCI delivery and industry-shaping innovation in Malaysia.

People and Competency Award

World Energy Supply Chain Awards

Our innovative IGNITE program was recognized by the Energy Industries Council (EIC) for empowering every employee to drive ideas that improve safety, quality, efficiency and sustainability, strengthening our culture of innovation across the business.

Health and Safety Initiative of the Year Award

Asian Oil & Gas Awards

We were honored in the Asian Oil & Gas Awards for our efforts to unite people, processes and tools to lead with proactive risk management, strong engagement and life-saving practices across all operations.

Excellence in Emiratization Award

Human Resources Middle East

We were recognized for our commitment to Emiratization and creating meaningful roles for UAE nationals, investing in their development and building an inclusive environment where Emirati talent grows, leads and thrives.

Most Premier Fully Integrated Provider of Engineering & Construction Solutions, Malaysia 2025

World Business Outlook

We were awarded for our strength delivering end-to-end engineering and construction solutions, recognizing our streamlined interfaces, improved certainty, reduced risk and integrated project execution that sets the bar for the industry.

Best Recruitment Strategy Award

Employee Happiness Awards, UAE

Our Offshore Middle East Talent Acquisition team earned this award for building a standout recruitment strategy that attracts top talent, elevates candidate experience and supports our people-first culture. The team was also named a finalist for the HR Team of the Year Award.

Mahesh Swaminathan, Oil and Gas Executive of the Year 2025

Asian Management Excellence Awards

Mahesh Swaminathan, our Senior Vice President, Subsea and Floating Facilities, was recognized for his leadership of the business line through transformative growth delivering complex offshore projects, strengthening local partnerships and driving innovation that supports energy security across Asia and Australasia.

Best Offshore Engineering Solutions Provider, Malaysia 2025

World Business Outlook

This recognition reflects our deepwater expertise and global reach—from complex pipelaying to major jacket fabrications—proving our ability to execute technically demanding offshore projects with precision, reliability and innovation.
