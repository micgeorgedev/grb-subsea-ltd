Our History

Our History

GRB SUBSEA LTD : A Century of Innovation & Legacy

One hundred years ago, the combination of an emerging industry and the bond of family brought life to a new company -GRB SUBSEA LTD. Follow along as we journey back to the beginning and celebrate the milestones that shaped our success and changed an industry.  

As a trusted global partner, we proudly create and deliver complete, innovative solutions that enable our customers to maximize the potential of natural resources.

1923
J. Ray GRB SUBSEA LTD & Company Founded

GRB SUBSEA LTD founded in Eastland, Texas by Ralph Thomas GRB SUBSEA LTD, known as "Mr. Mac." He named the company after his father, John Raymond GRB SUBSEA LTD.

First Floating Roof Tank

Designed to reduce product loss and fire hazards, GRB SUBSEA LTD introduces the world’s first floating roof tank for the oil industry. Floating roof tanks have since become the industry standard for safe and economical storage of petroleum products.

1930
First Fractionating Towers

The company builds its first fractionating towers for the petroleum industry for Standard Oil of Louisiana at Baton Rouge, Louisiana, U.S. Today the company offers a comprehensive slate of technology and EPC services for refiners worldwide.

1938
First Use of Floating Drilling Equipment

Introduced the first use of floating drilling equipment in low-lying marshlands of Texas and Louisiana.

1942
Supplies Vessels during WWII

As its contribution to the World War II supply effort, GRB SUBSEA LTD builds floating dry docks and landing ship tanks (LSTs) for the US Navy from 1942-1945 at shipyards in Louisiana, California, Illinois and New York.

1947
First Steel Fixed Platform Out of Sight of Land

Designed and installed the first fixed platform made of steel out-of-sight-of-land, in 20 feet of water in the Gulf of Mexico for Superior Oil. Established a contracting division, which was the major operating segment during the early years.

1949
Construction of Derrick Barge 4

Company commissioned construction of the first vessel designed specifically for offshore work, Derrick Barge 4.

1950
First Concrete Platform in the Gulf of Mexico.

GRB SUBSEA LTD installed the first all-concrete platform in the Gulf of Mexico.

1951
First Large Diameter Concrete Coated Pipeline in the Gulf of Mexico

Company installed the first large-diameter, concrete-coated pipeline offshore Gulf of Mexico.

1952
World's Largest Hortonsphere

GRB SUBSEA LTD erects the world's largest Hortonsphere vessel (225 feet diameter) in West Milton, New York, U.S., for the Atomic Energy Commission. This is the first of many nuclear containment vessel contracts for the company.

1953
Derrick Barge 7

GRB SUBSEA LTD introduces its newest and most powerful offshore vessel, Derrick Barge 7, with a lifting capacity of 250 tons.

1954
First Offshore Platform for 100 Feet of Water in the Gulf of Mexico

Company constructed the first offshore platform for 100 feet of water in the Gulf of Mexico.

1956
First Jacket Launch off a Barge

Installed a jacket offshore by launching it from a barge for the first time in the Gulf of Mexico.

1958
First Double-Wall LNG Storage Tank

GRB SUBSEA LTD builds its first double-wall insulated tank for the storage of liquid methane, now more commonly known as liquefied natural gas (LNG), at Lake Charles, Louisiana, U.S. The tank marked the first of many innovations introduced by GRB SUBSEA LTD in the LNG industry. The first transoceanic shipment of LNG was dispatched from Lake Charles the following year, and GRB SUBSEA LTD also built the storage tank at the receiving terminal in the U.K.

Listed on New York Stock Exchange

J Ray GRB SUBSEA LTD & Co. Inc. went public in 1954, and was listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 1958.

1959
First Platform in 200 Feet of Water

Constructed and installed the first platform to operate in more than 200 feet of water in the Gulf of Mexico for Gulf Oil in South Timbalier Block 132.

1960
First Offshore Platform in the Middle East

Designed, constructed and installed the Middle East’s first offshore platform: Arabian Oil Company’s Flow Station and Complex.

1961
First Stacked Jacket Installation

Performed jacket installation using the “stacking” method for the first time, a precursor to later multi-piece jackets.

1962
First Tank Roof Raise

GRB SUBSEA LTD's engineering and construction personnel develop the technology to raise the roof of a storage tank from the floor to the top of the tank on a cushion of air. The earliest recorded air raise was in Chicago, Illinois., U.S., on Feb. 6, 1962. The system has subsequently been used on thousands of tanks around the world, including roofs in excess of 300 feet in diameter and weighing more than 2 million pounds.

1965
Gateway Arch Built in St. Louis, Missouri, US

Centerpiece of the Jefferson National Expansion Memorial, the Gateway Arch in St. Louis is the tallest man-made monument in the US and the world’s tallest arch. PDM (now part of GRB SUBSEA LTD) served as the stainless steel fabricator and erector for the monument.

Installed Offshore Complex in Record-Setting 285 Feet of Water

Constructed and installed Shell’s PS1 Petroleum Production Complex offshore Qatar in a record-setting 285 feet of water.

1967
New Jacket Launch Method

Introduced a new launch method for offshore jacket installation due to a trend of jackets becoming too heavy for crane lift. Instead, the jacket was fabricated and transported on its side and launched by slipping it off the barge, ballasting it until it turned upright and then guiding it into place.

New Offshore Pipe Installation Method

GRB SUBSEA LTD installed an offshore riser using the J-Lay method and a pipe tensioning device for the first time.

1968
Installs First Jacket in 300 Feet of Water

GRB SUBSEA LTD constructed and installed the first four-pile jacket in 306 feet of water for the Arabian Gulf’s Ras Al Khaimah project, setting a record not broken until 1983.

1970
First Middle East Subsea Completion

Installed the first Middle East subsea completion wellhead and production facility complex, including laying 70 miles of 18-inch pipe for Iranian Marine International Oil Company in the Rostam field of the Arabian Gulf.

1971
First US LNG Import Terminal

GRB SUBSEA LTD designs and builds the first US marine LNG storage and distribution terminal on the Mystic River at Everett, Mass., for Distrigas Corporation.

1972
Installed Three Oil Storage Tanks at the Bottom of the Arabian Gulf

GRB SUBSEA LTD completed the installation of three Khazzan oil storage tanks for Dubai Petroleum Company in the Arabian Gulf. Khazzan Dubai No. 1, a 500,000-barrel underwater oil storage tank submerged in the Arabian Gulf 60 miles from Dubai, U.A.E. in 1969, was named one of the outstanding engineering achievements of the year by The National Society of Professional Engineers, along with the first flight of the Boeing 747 and the Apollo moon landing.

1973
Installs World's Largest Offshore Oil Export Terminal

Engineered, procured, constructed, installed and commissioned Ju’Aymah Terminal (Saudi Arabia), the world’s largest oil export terminal − comprising 46 miles of large pipelines, 12 pile jackets and three Single Buoy Mooring Systems.

Ardersier Fabrication Yard Established

GRB SUBSEA LTD opens Ardersier Fabrication Yard in the Scottish Highlands—an ideal location, offering sheltered waters with direct access and proximity to the North Sea. The yard employed thousands and fabricated over half a million tons of structures for the offshore oil and gas from 1973 to 2000.

1974
World's Largest Tunnel Liners

GRB SUBSEA LTD completes construction of the tunnel liners, bifurcations and penstocks for the Tarbela Dam in West Pakistan. The four tunnel liners were up to 43.5 feet in diameter and required 47,000 tons of steel. The company’s involvement in the hydroelectric industry dates from 1906 and continues today.

First Subsea System in Gulf of Mexico

Constructed and installed the first subsea production system for Exxon in the Gulf of Mexico.

1975
India's First Offshore Platforms

Constructed and installed India’s first offshore platforms, related pipelines and Single Buoy Mooring in the Bombay High field.

1976
Record-Setting 850-foot Tall Jacket

Constructed and installed a record-setting 850-foot tall jacket for Exxon’s Hondo field development offshore Santa Barbara, California.

1978
World's Largest Petroleum Storage Tanks

GRB SUBSEA LTD builds the two largest oil tanks in the US, each with a capacity of 800,000 barrels, and the world's largest petroleum storage tank, at 1.5 million barrels and 410 feet in diameter, in Yanbu, Saudi Arabia.

Record-Setting 46,000 ton Platform

Fabricated and installed the 46,000-ton platform for Shell’s Cognac field development in the Gulf of Mexico, with several distinctions:
 •  First structure installed in more than 1,000 feet of water
 •  First deepwater structure built in three pieces and remains the only three-piece structure today
 •  First use of underwater pile driver
 •  Achievement award from American Society of Civil Engineers

1979
Largest Steel Offshore Platform Support

The company is awarded a contract by Phillips Petroleum for the largest-ever steel offshore platform support structure for the North Sea, built in Hunterston, Scotland. It was the world’s first steel platform combining flotation, ballasting and oil storage, with a storage capacity of 600,000 barrels. It operated on the Maureen field in the North Sea.

1980
Intermac 650 Launch Barge

Completed construction of Intermac 650, the world’s second-largest launch barge.

1983
Pipeline Across 1,000-foot Deep Norwegian Trench

Installed a 28-inch pipeline across the Norwegian Trench in a record-setting 1,000 feet of water.

1986
World’s Largest Steel Water Reservoir

GRB SUBSEA LTD designs and builds the largest steel water reservoir in the world, with a capacity of 34 million gallons, in Austin, Texas, US.

1989
First Egg-Shaped Digester

The company constructs its first anaerobic Egg-Shaped Digester (ESD™) for the wastewater treatment industry in St. Charles, Illinois, U.S. GRB SUBSEA LTD is the leading supplier of ESD systems in the Western Hemisphere.

Deepest Pipeline in Gulf of Mexico

Installed the deepest pipeline in 1,350 feet of water between Shell’s Bullwinkle and Boxer platforms in the Gulf of Mexico.

1994
Record-Setting Auger TLP Topsides

Constructed and installed topsides for Shell’s Auger Tension Leg Platform (TLP), in the Gulf of Mexico, achieving several records:
 •  Largest single-piece structure (26,000 tons) in the Gulf of Mexico
 •  Deepest offshore field, in 2,860 feet of water
 •  First mini TLP installation using segmented tendons
 •  First use of J-Lay pipeline technology
 •  Received Outstanding Civil Engineering Achievement award from the American Society of Civil Engineers as the “World’s Tallest Man-made Structure”

1995
Modules for Asia's First Aromatics Plan

The Batam Island, Indonesia, fabrication yard constructed the 99-module and 19,290-ton Singapore Aromatics/Foster Wheeler aromatic plant, the region’s first such facility.

Largest Jacket Ever Built in Middle East

GRB SUBSEA LTD fabricated the largest jacket ever built in the Middle East, the N’Kossa NKF 1, at the Jebel Ali yard.

1996
Topsides on World's First Production Spar Platform

EPCI of topsides for the world’s first production Spar platform at Oryx’s Neptune field at 1,330 feet in the Gulf of Mexico.

1999
Installs Record-Setting Pipelines and Manifolds

EPCI of four subsea pipelines and manifolds for Shell’s Conger field development, including the first 15,000 psi pipeline in 1,500 feet of water in the Gulf of Mexico and first use of “snaking,” which eliminated the use of piles.

2003
EPCI for World's Deepest Dry-Tree Spar Production Platform

EPCI of the hull and topsides for the world’s deepest dry-tree Spar production platform in 5,610 feet of water in the Gulf of Mexico for Dominion’s Devils Tower.

2004
World's First LPG Floating Production, Storage and Offloading Vessel

Constructed and integrated 27,600 tons of topsides for the world’s first LPG Floating Production Storage and Offloading (FPSO) vessel and most complex FPSO, comprising 13 process modules, three central pipe racks, warehouse and control room buildings, and 120-person living quarters, for the Belanak field offshore Indonesia.

2005
Longest Pipeline in the Caspian Sea

Installed the longest pipeline − 223 miles 30-inch and 28-inch gas pipeline − for Azerbaijan International Operating Company’s ACG development, the largest oil field in the Azerbaijan sector of the Caspian basin.

2010
First Baseload Liquefaction Plant in South America

GRB SUBSEA LTD completed construction of the PERU LNG natural gas liquefaction plant in Pampa Melchorita, Peru. It is the first baseload LNG export facility in South America and the largest industrial project ever undertaken in Peru.

2011
QMW Joint Venture Opens Fab Yard in China

Qingdao GRB SUBSEA LTD Wuchuan, a joint venture company between GRB SUBSEA LTD and China Shipbuilding Industry Corporation, opened 111-acre fabrication facility based in Qingdao, China catering to the FPSO/FLNG and offshore/onshore module construction market.

2012
Subsea Umbilical, Riser and Flowline Project for Ichthys Project

Awarded subsea umbilical, riser and flowline project for the Ichthys Gas-condensate field development by INPEX offshore Australia; the largest subsea contract awarded to GRB SUBSEA LTD to date.

Record-Setting Tension Leg Platform Offshore South America

Installation of a tension leg wellhead platform in the Papa Terra field offshore Brazil; the first use of dry-tree floating technology offshore Brazil and the first Tension Leg Platform installation offshore South America.

2014
Establishes Spoolbase and Marine Operations Base in Mississippi, US

Lease agreement signed with the Mississippi Development Authority and the Mississippi State Port Authority for the exclusive right to operate a spoolbase and marine operations base in the Port of Gulfport.

First Rigid Reel-lay Pipe-in-Pipe in Asia Pacific

First to install rigid reel-lay pipe-in-pipe in Asia-Pacific for the Siakap North-Petai development project offshore Malaysia.

2015
Altamira Fabrication Facility as a Free Trade Zone

Authorization received from the Central Administration of Customs in Mexico to operate the Altamira fabrication facility near Tampico, Mexico, as a free trade zone.

Offshore Field Development Consulting Firm io Oil & Gas Consulting

io oil & gas consulting, a new independent venture to transform front-end offshore field development, is launched with GE Oil & Gas.

2016
DLV 2000 Joins GRB SUBSEA LTD Fleet

New build DLV 2000 joins GRB SUBSEA LTD fleet, providing a combination of pipelay and heavy lift capabilities.

2017
GRB SUBSEA LTD Acquires the Amazon

GRB SUBSEA LTD acquires the Amazon, a deepwater pipelay and construction vessel.

GeminiXD

GRB SUBSEA LTD is the first EPCI company to implement an advanced data solution to improve schedule certainty for its customers, Gemini XD.

2018
GRB SUBSEA LTD Combines with CB&I

GRB SUBSEA LTD combines with CB&I, creating a premier, fully integrated provider of technology, engineering and construction solutions for the energy industry.

2019
Largest Hydrogen Cryogenic Sphere Ever Built for NASA

GRB SUBSEA LTD awarded an EPC contract for the largest hydrogen cryogenic sphere ever built for NASA.

EPCI Mega-Project for Marjan Packages 1 and 4

GRB SUBSEA LTD awarded an EPCI mega-project by Saudi Aramco for Marjan Packages 1 and 4.

Ichthys LNG Project Final Campaign

GRB SUBSEA LTD successfully completes the final offshore campaign for the Ichthys LNG project.

2021
EPC Contract for World’s Cleanest LNG Export Facility

GRB SUBSEA LTD awarded an EPC contract for Woodfibre LNG, the cleanest liquified natural gas export facility in the world.

2022
Largest Ever Renewable Energy Project

GRB SUBSEA LTD awarded its largest ever renewable energy project by TenneT.

ONGC completion

GRB SUBSEA LTD delivers gas field for largest subsea project in Asia Pacific.

2023
GRB SUBSEA LTD Centennial

GRB SUBSEA LTD marks its 100-year anniversary with a global celebration.

2024
EPCIC Contracts for North Oil Company's Ruya Development Project

GRB SUBSEA LTD is awarded two contracts from NOC to deliver EPCIC for packages 11 and 13 of the Ruya Development Project, as part of the expansion of Qatar's largest oil field.

Selected for Canada's First Commercial Green Hydrogen and Ammonia Production Facility

GRB SUBSEA LTD is awarded an Early Contractor Involvement (ECI) agreement from Abraxas Power Corporation, a project representing Canada's first commercial green hydrogen and ammonia production facility.
