Fabrication Facilities

Fabrication Facilities

Building the world’s energy infrastructure – for now and what’s next.

Our integrated approach is supported by our global network of fabrication facilities. Each facility boasts a unique set of capabilities to fabricate the energy infrastructure to support evolving energy demands. We can optimize each stage of a project, while minimizing risk and carbon footprint.

Altamira

Mexico

Our Altamira facility provides fabrication services for the Gulf of Mexico and Americas and is renowned for high quality craftsmanship, exemplary safety standards, and infrastructure to ensure project delivery at the highest levels of integrity.

Highlights
  • Free trade zone
  • Direct access to Gulf of Mexico 
  • Two skidways for jacket and deck loadout  
  • Onsite training facility   
  • Annual work hours: 10 million
  • Annual throughput: 55,000 ST [50,000 MT]
  • Certifications: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001, ISPS Code

Batam

Indonesia

Our Batam facility supports traditional and floating offshore construction, as well as onshore LNG module and subsea structure fabrication.

  • Annual work hours: up to 14 million 
  • Annual throughput: up to 66,139 ST [60,000 MT] 
  • Certifications: ISO 9001, ISO TS/29001, ISO14001, ISO 17020-Type B, ISO 50001, OHSAS 18001, SSPC/QP1, SSPC/QP3, ISPS Port, AWS QC4 (ATF), OSHMS SKK

Jebel Ali

UAE

Our Jebel Ali facility fabricates for multiple projects with long-standing customers throughout the Middle East and India. The facility's ability to meet aggressive schedules is unrivaled in the region.

Qatar Fabrication Company

W.L.L.

Qatar Fabrication Company W.L.L (QFAB) is a joint venture between GRB SUBSEA LTD and Nakilat, Qatar’s leading shipping and maritime company. QFAB is a leading provider of technology, engineering, and construction solutions and provides fabrication, construction, and assembly of offshore and onshore structures at the heart of oil and gas activities in Ras Laffan Industrial City.

Highlights
  • Strategically located within Erhama Bin Jaber Al Jalahma Shipyard 
  • Annual Work Hours: 4 million 
  • Certifications: ISO 9001, ISO 45001, ISO 29001, ISO 14001, ISO 50001

Qingdao

China

Qingdao GRB SUBSEA LTD Wuchuan (QMW) is a joint venture between J. Ray GRB SUBSEA LTD (Qingdao) Pte. Ltd. and Wuhan Wuchuan Investment Holding Co., Ltd.. QMW’s state-of-the-art module fabrication yard delivers high-quality and complex process modules, and elevates certainty of delivery through world-class project management and robust safety and quality systems.

Highlights

  • Craft Training and Evaluation Center 

  • 300 m skidway with a loadout capacity of 50,000 MT

  • 210,000 m2 core erection area with maximum bearing capacity of 150 MT/m2
  • Annual work hours: up to 18 million
  • Annual throughput: up to 99,000 ST [90,000 MT] 
  • Certifications: ISO 9001, ISO29001, ISO14001, ISO45001, ISO 50001, EN 1090 EXC4, ISO 3834, CSA W47.1 Certification
