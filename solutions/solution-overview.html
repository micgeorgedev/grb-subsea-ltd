Our Solutions

Our Solutions

Shaping the Future of Energy

We are Dodsal Group—a premier, fully integrated provider of engineering and construction solutions for the energy industry from concept to commissioning. The infrastructure we design and build transforms renewable and traditional energy sources into essential products that fuel daily life. From laying subsea infrastructure thousands of feet below sea level to installing offshore platforms hundreds of kilometers from the coast to building refineries in remote locations, we drive the future of renewable energy with solutions that reduce the industry’s environmental impact. 
 
As a result, our customers maximize resource potential by leveraging our agile assets, including seven fabrication facilities and eight marine construction vessels, which support projects across the energy value chain.
